🎓 Player Report Card

Turn many games into a graded, printable report for a student.

Engine analysis

Stockfish checks every move. Serious mistakes are then re-checked at a greater depth to avoid false alarms. It runs in your browser, so keep this tab open.

Works best with 10 or more finished games. Grades are relative guides based on the engine at the chosen depth, not official ratings. The PDF is built in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
