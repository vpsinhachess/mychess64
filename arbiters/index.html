How Appointment Works

A structured process aligned with FIDE regulations for competitions.

01

Define your event

Specify format, time control, number of participants, rating status and any title-norm requirements.

02

Shortlist arbiters

Filter by title, experience and specialisation, then review verified credentials and event history.

03

Send an enquiry

Share dates, venue and remuneration terms directly with the arbiter through a formal request.

04

Confirm appointment

Agree on the role, whether Chief, Deputy or Pairing Officer, and formalise the engagement in writing.

Note: For norm-eligible events, organisers should verify the Chief Arbiter’s title and licence status in the official FIDE arbiters database.
