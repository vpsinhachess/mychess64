Chess Diagram Generator

Create a chess diagram from a FEN or by dragging pieces. Add arrows, highlights and a caption, then export as PNG, JPG or SVG.

Position

Pieces & tools

Drag pieces to move them, or pick a piece and tap squares to place it. Drag a piece off the board to remove it. Right-drag (or use the arrow tool) to draw arrows; right-click a square for a circle.

Style

Export

Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded. Every export includes the MyChess64 logo and the side to move. Pieces: “cburnett” by Colin M. L. Burnett (GPL, BSD, GFDL), as used on Lichess.

How to use the Chess Diagram Generator

Make a clean chess diagram for a lesson, article, newsletter or social post. Set up any position, mark it up with arrows and highlights, and export it as an image. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded.

  1. Enter a FEN, or press Start and drag pieces onto the board.
  2. Add arrows, highlight squares and write a caption if you need one.
  3. Choose a board theme, the size (480 to 1440 px) and the format: PNG, JPG or SVG.
  4. Download the image, or use Copy image, Copy link or Print.

Chess Diagram Generator FAQ

Which image formats can I export?

PNG, JPG and SVG (vector).

Is the diagram uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded.

Can I share the position as a link?

Yes. Use Copy link to get a link to the position.
