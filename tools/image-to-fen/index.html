Image-to-FEN Converter

Turn a board screenshot into a position, then correct it.

1. Drag a square around the board on the image (corner to corner). 2. Detect pieces. 3. Fix any mistakes on the board on the right.

Detection finds which squares are occupied and each piece’s colour. It cannot tell piece types apart, so occupied squares start as pawns. Pick a piece, then click a square to correct it. A standard starting position is recognised automatically.

Everything is processed in your browser. Your image is never uploaded. Piece artwork: "cburnett" by Colin M. L. Burnett (GPL, BSD, GFDL), the set used on Lichess.

How to use the Image-to-FEN Converter

Have a chessboard screenshot and want it as a FEN? Mark the board on the image, let the tool find the occupied squares, then correct the pieces on an editable board. Your image is processed in your browser and never uploaded.

  1. Choose a board screenshot, then drag a square around the board on the image (corner to corner), or press Use whole image.
  2. Tick Black at bottom in image if the board is shown from Black’s side, then press Detect pieces.
  3. Fix any mistakes on the board on the right: pick a piece, then click a square.
  4. Choose the side to move and press Copy FEN.

Image-to-FEN Converter FAQ

Does it recognise every piece automatically?

No. Detection finds which squares are occupied and each piece’s colour, but it cannot tell piece types apart. Occupied squares start as pawns and you correct them on the board.

Is my image uploaded?

No. Everything is processed in your browser.

What if the screenshot shows the starting position?

A standard starting position is recognised automatically.
