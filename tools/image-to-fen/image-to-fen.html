Image-to-FEN Converter

Turn a board screenshot into a position, then correct it.

1. Drag a square around the board on the image (corner to corner). 2. Detect pieces. 3. Fix any mistakes on the board on the right.

Detection finds which squares are occupied and each piece’s colour. It cannot tell piece types apart, so occupied squares start as pawns. Pick a piece, then click a square to correct it. A standard starting position is recognised automatically.

Open in Analysis BoardShare position

Everything is processed in your browser. Your image is never uploaded. Piece artwork: "cburnett" by Colin M. L. Burnett (GPL, BSD, GFDL), the set used on Lichess.
