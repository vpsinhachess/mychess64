PGN–FEN–SAN–UCI Converter

Convert between chess notation formats.

Examples: paste 1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 as SAN, or e2e4 e7e5 g1f3 as UCI, or a full PGN. A lone FEN lists its legal moves in both notations.

How to use the PGN–FEN–SAN–UCI Converter

Different chess programs speak different notations. This converter turns a game written as PGN, SAN moves or UCI moves into any of the others, and can output the final FEN or a FEN after every move.

  1. Paste your input. Leave Input is on Detect automatically, or choose PGN, SAN moves, UCI moves or FEN.
  2. Choose what to Convert to: PGN, SAN moves, UCI moves, FEN (final position), FEN after every move, or Everything.
  3. If the game does not start from the usual position, add a Starting FEN (optional).
  4. Copy the output, or use Download .txt.

PGN–FEN–SAN–UCI Converter FAQ

What is the difference between SAN and UCI?

SAN is the human-readable move notation used in books and PGN, for example Nf3. UCI writes the from and to squares, for example g1f3, and is what chess engines use.

What does a lone FEN give me?

Pasting a single FEN lists its legal moves in both notations.

Can I start from a custom position?

Yes. Use the optional Starting FEN field.
