Chess Position Share Tool

Share a position as a link or an image.

The link stores the position in the address itself, so nothing is saved on a server. Anyone who opens it sees the same board. Piece artwork: "cburnett" by Colin M. L. Burnett (GPL, BSD, GFDL), the set used on Lichess.

How to use the Chess Position Share Tool

Send a chess position to a student, a friend or a group chat. Enter a FEN and the tool gives you a link that opens the same board, or a board image you can download or copy. Nothing is saved on a server.

  1. Enter the position as a FEN and, if you like, add a title and arrows.
  2. Choose a board Theme and whether White or Black is at the bottom.
  3. Use Copy link or Share…, or Download image or Copy image.
  4. To keep working on the position, open it in the Analysis Board, Lichess or Chess.com, or edit it in the Diagram Generator.

Chess Position Share Tool FAQ

Where is the position stored?

In the link itself. Nothing is saved on a server, and anyone who opens the link sees the same board.

Can I share an image instead of a link?

Yes. Use Download image or Copy image.

Can I continue analysing the position?

Yes. There are shortcuts to the Analysis Board, Lichess and Chess.com, and to the Diagram Generator.
