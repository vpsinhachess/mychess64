FEN Validator

Check a FEN field by field, plus whether the position is legal.

Checks the six FEN fields and whether the position could arise in a real game. Warnings do not make a FEN invalid. Piece artwork: "cburnett" by Colin M. L. Burnett (GPL, BSD, GFDL), the set used on Lichess.

How to use the FEN Validator

A FEN string describes a chess position in one line. This validator checks each of the six FEN fields and whether the position could arise in a real game, and shows the position on a board so you can see what the FEN means.

  1. Paste or type your FEN into the box, or press Starting position to try the standard setup.
  2. Read the field-by-field results and the summary on the right.
  3. Press Apply suggested fixes to correct what can be corrected automatically, then Copy FEN.

FEN Validator FAQ

What are the six FEN fields?

Piece placement, side to move, castling rights, en passant square, halfmove clock and fullmove number.

Does a warning mean my FEN is invalid?

No. Warnings do not make a FEN invalid; they point out things that are unusual.

Does it check that the position is legal?

Yes. It also checks whether the position could arise in a real game.
