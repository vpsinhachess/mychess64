Chess GIF & Video Maker

Turn a PGN game into an animated replay.

GIFs use a fixed 216-colour palette, which suits flat board graphics. Video is recorded live in your browser as WebM, so keep this tab open while it records. Piece artwork: "cburnett" by Colin M. L. Burnett (GPL, BSD, GFDL), the set used on Lichess.

How to use the Chess GIF & Video Maker

Create an animated replay of a chess game to use in lessons, posts and messages. Paste a PGN, preview the animation, then create a GIF or record a WebM video, all inside your browser.

  1. Paste your PGN into the box.
  2. Choose the Size, Seconds per move and board Theme, and tick View from Black’s side or Show coordinates if you want them.
  3. Press Preview to watch it.
  4. Press Create GIF, or Create video (WebM).

Chess GIF & Video Maker FAQ

What is the difference between the GIF and the video?

The GIF uses a fixed 216-colour palette, which suits flat board graphics. The video is recorded live in your browser as WebM.

Why must I keep the tab open for video?

The video is recorded live in your browser, so the tab has to stay open while it records.

Does my PGN leave my device?

No. Both the GIF and the video are made in your browser.
