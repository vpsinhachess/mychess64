Questions, answered

A few things to know

Who is MyChess64 Academy for?

Chess students, teachers and coaches who want a dedicated environment for lessons, guided practice and learning activities.

What happens in a private chess classroom?

A private classroom supports focused coaching, position-based explanations and learning activities between a teacher and student. Available tools depend on the connected classroom application.

Can a coach give personalised homework?

Personalised homework is a core part of the learning experience. Assignment creation and progress tracking are available once connected to your classroom system.

Can I use the academy on my phone?

Yes. The site adapts to desktop, tablet and mobile screens. Test the classroom on the devices you plan to support.
