Frequently Asked Questions

Is MyChess64 free to use?

Yes. All MyChess64 Tools and Utilities, including the FEN validator, PGN viewer, rating calculators, scoresheet and certificate generators, chess books, news and champion lists, are free with no login. The Digital Chess Clock and Play with Computer, Friend or Self modes are free too. Lessons, live play and the coach, arbiter and organiser tools need a role-based login.

How can I learn chess online as a beginner?

Open the Student Panel, log in, and start with Chess Basics. Then continue with Opening Repertoire, Middlegame Training and Endgame Training to improve step by step.

Can I play chess online with friends or the computer?

Yes. The Player Panel lets you play live chess, play against the computer, challenge a friend, play both sides yourself, or start from a custom position.

Is there a MyChess64 app?

Yes. Get MyChess64 for Android (Google Play or direct APK), iPhone and iPad (App Store), Windows (Microsoft Store), or install it from your browser on any device.

Who can use the Coach, Arbiter and Organiser panels?

Chess coaches, tournament arbiters and event organisers get dedicated tools such as PGN editors, student trackers, Swiss pairing, time planners and prize fund splitters.
