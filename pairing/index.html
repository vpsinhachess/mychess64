Standings (round wise)

FIDE rules applied (Swiss)
  • Two players never meet twice; forfeited games do not count as played.
  • Pairing inside score groups: top half (S1) against bottom half (S2) by rating. The search first minimises the number of players floated to another score group, then the number of players who do not get their colour preference. If no legal pairing exists, colour rules are relaxed and you are told.
  • Buchholz ignores unplayed rounds (no virtual opponents).
  • Pairing-allocated bye (1 pt) goes to the lowest-ranked player of the lowest score group who has no bye or forfeit win yet.
  • Colours: nobody gets the same colour 3 times in a row or a colour difference above 2; two players with the same absolute preference are not paired; the stronger preference is served first; colour is alternated otherwise.
  • Last round: players scoring above 50% may be given an unfavourable colour (top-scorer exception).
  • Forfeits give the full point to the present player, no colours and no Buchholz credit.
  • Late entrants receive 0, ½ or 1 entry point and are paired from the next round. Absent players are not paired and receive 0, ½ or 1 point (arbiter's choice). Withdrawn players are never paired again.
